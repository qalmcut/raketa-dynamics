About Raketa Dynamics

Precision aerospace replicas and technical aviation projects based in Berkshire, United Kingdom.

Detail beyond the silhouette.

Raketa Dynamics focuses on the details that make an aircraft recognisable to people who know it closely.

Cockpit reproduction, panel lines, surface treatment, weathering, markings, configuration and presentation all contribute to the finished collector piece.

The models are intended for serious collectors, aviation enthusiasts and people with a particular connection to the aircraft represented.

Highly detailed aircraft replicas for collectors and aviation enthusiasts.
